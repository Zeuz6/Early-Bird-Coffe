Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We Have Answers.

Here are some common questions about Early Bird Coffee, our products, shipping, and ordering.
What products does Early Bird Coffee sell?

We currently offer traditional coffee grounds and a 25-count package of Keurig-compatible coffee pods.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days. Standard shipping is estimated to take 3–7 business days.

Can I return or exchange an order?

Unopened products may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Please contact customer support before sending a return.

Are the coffee pods compatible with Keurig machines?

Yes. Our coffee pods are designed for use with compatible Keurig-style coffee makers.

Is this website a real online store?

Early Bird Coffee is a student e-commerce project. The checkout system is for demonstration purposes only.

Customer Information

Policies, Privacy & Security

We want customers to understand how ordering, privacy, and website security are handled.

Business Policies

Orders are processed after confirmation and are normally prepared within 1–2 business days.

Unopened products may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Customers should contact support before beginning a return or exchange.

Shipping times are estimates and may vary. Because this website is a class project, checkout and payment functions are for demonstration purposes only.

Privacy Policy

Early Bird Coffee respects customer privacy. Information entered on this website, such as an email address submitted through the newsletter form, is only used for the purpose described on the site.

We do not sell personal information to third parties. This class project does not intentionally collect or store sensitive payment information.

Security

Our website uses HTTPS to help protect information transmitted between visitors and the site.

Customers should always make sure they are using the official Early Bird Coffee website before entering information online.

Since this is a demonstration website, no real credit card payment information should be entered or submitted.
